# Third-Party Launchers

When using third-party launchers, it's important to know that not all launchers work with FancyMenu. Some launchers have the terrible habit to inject stuff into the game, sometimes in bad/unoptimized ways. Some launchers even change the GUI code, which is bascially the worst case when you want FancyMenu to work properly.

For "clients", it's a bit different. Clients are a game breaker (pun intended) in basically all cases. Clients are almost always heavily modify the game, and its GUI, which makes FancyMenu stop working. It is absolutely **not recommended to use clients** when using FancyMenu.

# Choosing The Right Launcher

You don't have to live without the QoL features that launchers give you. There are multiple well-made launchers that work perfectly fine with FancyMenu.

## CurseForge Launcher

The CurseForge launcher is probably the best launcher for when you just want easy account and modpack/instance organization. You just download the launcher, login to your Minecraft/Microsoft account, and then you can quickly create game instances with mods, or you download ready-to-start modpacks.

## Modrinth Launcher

Basically the same as the CurseForge launcher, but for Modrinth instead. Gives you easy account and modpack/instance organization.

# Bad Launchers & Clients

Some launchers are known to break FancyMenu, or at least make it less stable.

For clients, it's basically every client that is fully incompatible with FancyMenu, so please do not use them.

## Prism Launcher

Multiple users reported issues when using Prism, so even tho FancyMenu often works fine with Prism, it is very likely that there is some kind of incompatibility, even tho it does not always show.

## Lunar Client

This one is used a lot, that's why it is listed here even tho all clients are considered incompatible anyway. Lunar will break FancyMenu. Do not use both together.
