# Layers and Groups

Open the Layers widget through **Window -> Editor Widgets -> Layers**.

# Layer Order

Drag layers in the list to change their render order. You can also right-click an element and use **Move One Layer Up** or **Move One Layer Down**.

[Player Entity](./elements#player-entity) and [Item](./elements#item) elements can have rendering limits that prevent normal layer ordering.

# Groups

Groups organize related layers and let you move or hide them together.

- Create and rename groups.
- Rename individual layers.
- Drag layers into or out of groups.
- Drag a group to move all of its layers in the order.
- Collapse or expand a group.
- Show or hide every layer in a group.
- Delete a group without deleting its layers.

Group membership and collapsed state are saved with the layout.
